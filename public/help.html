What is PoI and why it matters

PoI (Proof of Intelligence) is not a casino or bookmaker. It is a system for measuring intelligence through real economic stakes.

Traditional platforms reward luck. PoI rewards knowledge. A participant who systematically makes correct predictions accumulates Proof of Intelligence — a cryptographic proof of intelligence recorded on the blockchain forever.

For regulators: The system is not a casino, bookmaker, or parimutuel. Here is why:

In a casino and at a bookmaker, mathematical expectation is always negative — the house always wins. In PoI, a smart participant has a positive mathematical expectation.

Unlike parimutuel (horse racing): parimutuel takes 15-25% of all bets as organizer profit. PoI takes only 0.5% from winning bets — and this money goes not to profit, but to operational costs: node operator rewards and gas. Treasury has no profit — everything returns to the community through GovernanceDAO.

The platform is fully decentralized. No central server, no owner, no possibility to manipulate results. The code is open, smart contracts are verified on the blockchain.

Why Batch Auction is the only correct model

Modern prediction markets use the double auction (order book) model, similar to NASDAQ. However, this model is fundamentally inapplicable to event markets.

The Fair Price Problem

Financial markets have arbitrage and a hedging curve. Event markets (weather, football, elections) have no such curve — it is impossible to determine fair price through arbitrage. This is why event betting has used parimutuel or bookmaker models for centuries — not double auction.

The Front-Running Problem

In order book systems a trader sees the order queue and can place an order before a large trade. With low liquidity this is catastrophic. In PoI all orders are collected over a period — all participants receive a single price (M*), front-running is physically impossible.

batch_interval = time_to_event / 30 × random(0.5, 1.5)

The random element prevents sniping — no one knows the exact time when the batch closes.

Self-financing Formulas

The system is fully self-financing — winnings are paid from the common pool of bets. State prices are determined by the following formulas:

Pi = Ti / (M − ΣYi)
ΣPi = 1
  • Pi — state price (probability of outcome i)
  • M — total pool (sum of all bets)
  • Ti — Pool Bet on outcome i (LP and seed only)
  • Yi — Fix Bet on outcome i (regular participants)

The condition ΣPi = 1 guarantees that the sum of all probabilities always equals 1.

Proof of Intelligence (PoI) and Proof of Conviction (PoC)

Proof of Intelligence (PoI) — Proof of Intelligence

PoI accumulates for every correct prediction. The less obvious the outcome you correctly predicted — the more PoI you receive.

If the prediction is correct:

score = log(1/p) − expected(p)

If the prediction is incorrect:

score = −log(1/(1−p)) − expected(p)

Where:

expected(p) = p·log(1/p) − (1−p)·log(1/(1−p))

Cumulative PoI:

PoI = Σ score_i

Here p — market price of the outcome at the time of your bet. Categories: Sports, Finance, Politics, Weather, Rare events.

Proof of Conviction (PoC) — Proof of Conviction

PoC combines intelligence with skin in the game — "put your skin in the game".

PoC = √stake × PoI

The square root prevents plutocracy: doubling the stake gives only 40% increase in weight.

Why do we need PoI and PoC?

  1. Right to participate — without PoI NFT you cannot place orders.
  2. Right to vote — voting in DisputeOracle is weighted by PoC. Your vote weighs proportionally to your proven intelligence.
  3. Verified resume — PoI NFT is proof of analytical abilities on the blockchain forever. Cannot be bought or faked.
Important: PoI is not awarded to Liquidity Provider — LP bets on all outcomes and makes no predictions.

Once a year — if Treasury has funds remaining after all expenses, ALL participants with PoC > 0 receive 70% of the remainder, distributed proportionally to their effective PoC. 30% carries over to the next year.

share = (your effective PoC / total effective PoC) × 70% of Treasury

Effective PoC includes an activity decay factor — inactive participants receive a reduced share:

  • ✅ Active this year → 100% of PoC
  • ⚠️ Inactive 1 year → 50% of PoC
  • ⚠️ Inactive 2 years → 25% of PoC
  • ❌ Inactive 3+ years → 12.5% of PoC

Note: PoC in the blockchain never changes — only the bonus calculation uses the decay factor.

share = (your PoC / total PoC of all participants) × 70% of Treasury

How to Place Orders

Minimum bet: 1 contract. Short selling is prohibited. You can only sell positions that have already been bought, and only after the first batch.

Only Fix Bet for regular participants. Pool Bet is available only for Liquidity Provider.

Fix Bet (Limit Order) For participants

You specify the outcome, number of contracts and minimum price. Winnings = 1 ETH per contract if the outcome is correct. If the market price at clearing exceeds your limit — the order is rejected and deposit is returned.

Pool Bet (Market Order) LP only

Bet on all outcomes proportionally to current prices. Available only for Liquidity Provider.

  1. Open batchpredict.ar.io in your browser — no installation needed. Works on phone and computer.
  2. Connect your Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet or any other)
  3. Register — mint PoI NFT ($2, includes first 1000 predictions)
  4. Select market and outcome
  5. Specify number of contracts (minimum 1) and limit price (from 0 to 1)
  6. Confirm in wallet — MetaMask will open automatically
  7. Wait for clearing — happens automatically on schedule
  8. Click Claim — after the event to collect winnings or deposit refund

Liquidity Provider (LP)

Important advantage: LP is not mandatory. In all existing decentralized prediction markets LP is mandatory — without it the market cannot exist. In PoI LP is not mandatory. Batch Auction works with any number of participants.

LP provides initial market liquidity. Bets on all outcomes simultaneously proportionally to current prices through Pool Bet.

How to become LP:

Click the "Become LP" button in the interface. The system will automatically distribute your bet across all outcomes.

LP Reward:

LP always wins on one outcome (the correct one) and loses on the rest. The win depends on the pool size (parimutuel), not fixed. Additionally LP receives 0.1% of their bet volume (maximum $100 per batch) as a liquidity premium.

LP Risk:

Losses on losing outcomes may exceed the gain on the correct outcome plus the 0.1% premium.

What LP does NOT receive:

  • ❌ PoI — LP makes no predictions
  • ❌ PoC — not awarded
  • ❌ Voting rights in DisputeOracle
  • ❌ LP does not run clearing

Clearing — how batch works

Clearing — the process of determining market prices and distributing winnings. Uses the Baron-Lange algorithm for batch auctions.

When does a batch start?

Batch starts automatically according to the formula:

batch_interval = time_to_event / 30 × random(0.5, 1.5)

Approximately 30 batches per market period. The random element prevents sniping — no one knows the exact time when the batch closes.

Important: A market is considered successful only if at least one batch has occurred. Final resolution without a batch does not pay out winnings.

Seed-ордера

When creating a market the AI agent places initial orders with probabilities from open sources. Seed provides initial liquidity but does not trigger clearing.

Security and Protection

💰 Where are funds stored?

Funds are never stored by a company. Here is the full flow:

  1. Participant places an order → ETH is sent to the TSS address
  2. TSS address holds funds until the final event resolution
  3. After event → top-5 PoC nodes sign the payout together → ETH goes to winners
  4. Rejected orders → participant clicks Claim → ETH returns to their wallet

The TSS address is not a company or server — it is a mathematically protected address whose key is split among the top-5 PoC nodes. No single person controls these funds.

  • 🔍
    Open source. Anyone can read every line. No hidden functions.
  • 🔑
    TSS (Threshold Signature Scheme). Participant funds are stored at the TSS address — the key is split among the top-5 PoC nodes. Signatures from all online nodes are required for payout. Even if one node is compromised — stealing funds is impossible without the rest.
  • 📍
    Winner addresses on the blockchain. Addresses are recorded on the blockchain forever when placing an order. Even with node collusion — it is impossible to redirect payment to another address.
  • 📊
    DA (Data Availability). All raw order data is published after each event. Any participant can verify the correctness of clearing and file a dispute.
  • 🛡️
    Node consensus. A dishonest node cannot change the result — majority of top-5 by PoC is required. A fraudster loses PoI NFT forever.
  • ♾️
    Arweave. Permanent immutable hash. File cannot be altered retroactively.
  • 📋
    Smart contract addresses are unique. A clone gets different addresses — your PoI remains only in the original.
